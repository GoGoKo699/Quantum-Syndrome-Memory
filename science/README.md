# Scientific account

The question is whether a small amount of *early* classical information about errors can replace the coherent storage of their nonorthogonal records. The result requires the following chain:

1. [Model and no-advice frontier](MODEL_AND_FRONTIER.md): precise memory cut, pure-ensemble Gram state, mixed-decomposition tradeoff, and the inherited helper boundary.
2. [Syndrome rigidity](SYNDROME_RIGIDITY.md): the syndrome pinches the Gram state; a homomorphism makes the pinching a small orbit of product phase patterns; sampling learns that orbit without creating coherence. The mixed-roof family (J_\eta) proves the whole frontier.
3. [Phase-channel consequence](PHASE_CHANNEL.md): error-record information becomes a quantum-transmission bound for arbitrary data codes, with the syndrome given free to both the early detector and final decoder.
4. [Interpretation and boundaries](INTERPRETATION.md): where the temporal resource claim applies and what it leaves open.

The [related-work map](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) distinguishes inherited tools from the proposed contribution. [Verification](../verification/README.md) records deterministic structural checks. The full arguments, rather than a separate manuscript file, live in the topic pages above.

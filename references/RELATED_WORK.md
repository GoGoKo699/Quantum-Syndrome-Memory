# Related work and attribution

The comparison separates the source-compression frontier and mathematical tools from the proposed sublinear-advice stability theorem. A targeted search of primary sources through 23 October 2026 did not locate the latter statement; that is a provisional novelty assessment, not a priority certificate.

| Source | Established result or closest question | Relationship here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hsieh and Watanabe, *Source Compression with a Quantum Helper* (2015)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.04366), Theorem 1, Eqs. (9)–(14) | A single-letter optimal asymptotic helper rate region with $R_1\ge H(X\mid U)$, $R_2\ge I(U;B)$. | We infer the dual reparametrization $G_{\mathcal E}(M)=M+A_{\mathcal E}(\chi-M)$ of its no-advice pure-ensemble frontier. This baseline is **inherited**. Their iid helper region alone does not bound the correlated syndrome-conditioned source. |
| [Devetak and Winter, *Distilling common randomness from bipartite quantum states* (2004)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0304196), Example 8, Theorem 9 | One-way common randomness and product-ensemble accessible-information additivity. | Foundational for the fully measured endpoint. A syndrome followed by a conditional measurement is a different, two-stage information task; a later measurement outcome is not freely reconciled in the one-way formulation. |
| [Winter and Yang, *Operational Resource Theory of Coherence* (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07975) | Coherence of formation and its resource properties. | The zero-memory Gram-state argument uses these coherence concepts. |
| [Pastushenko and Kronberg, *Improving the Performance of Quantum Cryptography by Using the Encryption of the Error Correction Data* (2023)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12347), §III | Syndrome disclosure changes the product prior and may change collective accessible information; a broader bound is discussed with a memory-time assumption. | Closest conceptual motivation. The present theorem answers a **narrower** fixed iid pure-record, sublinear homomorphism-advice question and does not solve their general QKD security problem. |
| [König, Renner, Bariska and Maurer, *Small Accessible Quantum Information Does Not Imply Security* (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.140502) ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0512021)) | General information-locking constructions. | Explains why a universal advice-bit count is not a substitute for the product-character proof. The constructions have different source and side-information structure. |
| [Beigi and Mehrabi, *From Qubit Tensorization to Gaussian Optimizers in Bosonic Systems* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28902), Lemma 31 and Appendix D | Binary entropy concavity in inverse form. | Supplies a mathematical ingredient for evaluating the explicit binary **no-advice** curve; no novelty is claimed for that lemma. |
| [Wilde, *Entanglement-of-formation potentials for feedback-assisted classical communication and randomness distillation* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04831) | Mixed roofs, retained quantum outputs, and feedback-assisted channel or randomness tasks. | A close methodological predecessor. The inspected theorems do not state the present product-character/homomorphism-advice stability bound. |
| [Koashi and Imoto, *What is Possible Without Disturbing Partially Known Quantum States?* (2002)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0101144) | Nondisturbing classical sectors of a pure-state source. | Context for early saturation of a reducible no-advice ensemble; no new irreducible-ensemble theorem is claimed. |
| [Devetak and Shor, *The capacity of a quantum channel for simultaneous transmission of classical and quantum information* (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0311131); [Devetak, *The private classical capacity and quantum capacity of a quantum channel* (2005)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0304127) | Degradable channels and coherent-information coding. | Background for the channel achievability; the arbitrary-code converse here is written directly using teleportation simulation and relative entropy. |

The proposed contribution is the **whole-frontier stability** of a fixed iid finite pure-record source against $o(n)$ predetermined homomorphism advice, together with a binary finite-block bound and its free-syndrome phase-channel consequence. It is distinct from the inherited baseline and from a general theorem about all classical advice. Related coherent readout architectures can motivate the memory cut, but no hardware-specific performance claim follows without a separate causal and resource analysis.
